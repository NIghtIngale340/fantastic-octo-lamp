# Justice — Character — solution

Answer: **Lady Justice**

Flag candidate: `pcgCTF{Lady Justice}`

Status: Answer independently supported by original evidence and public sources; not submitted or accepted on PCGCTF.

## Verification

1. Supplied image depicts a blindfolded woman with raised arms, scales in both hands and billowing robes in front of the courthouse windows.
2. U.S. General Services Administration’s official art collection identifies this sculpture at A. V. Bryan U.S.Courthouse as Justice Delayed, Justice Denied by Raymond Kaskey. Its description confirms that the depicted figure is Justice, blindfolded and holding scales in each hand.
3. Independently inspected Tim Evanson’s original photograph of the same statue, showing the same facial features, blindfold, hand grips, paired scales and drapery. Photographer calls the figure Blind Justice. For the requested character, Lady Justice is appropriate; the artwork title is a separate fact.
