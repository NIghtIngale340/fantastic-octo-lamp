# Justice — Character

**Platform:** PCGCTF · **Challenge ID:** 256 · **Category:** OSINT / IMINT · **Points:** 100

**Status:** Answer backed by the GSA artwork record. Exact PCGCTF submission not re-confirmed.

## The challenge

Identify the character represented by the blindfolded, scales-bearing statue.

## Solving it

The pose is the classic allegory — blindfold + balanced scales. The specific sculpture (at the A. V. Bryan U.S. Courthouse) is *Justice Delayed, Justice Denied* by Raymond Kaskey, but the question asks who the figure *represents*:

```text
GSA record: "Justice Delayed, Justice Denied" (Kaskey) — blindfolded Justice with scales
Character represented: Lady Justice
```
